▸ CORE FRAMEWORK — THE LATTICE

Grinberg proposed that space is not empty but a pre-physical, informational structure he called the Lattice — a continuous space-time-energy matrix permeating all of reality. The brain does not passively receive the world. It actively generates experience by interacting with that field, distorting the Lattice to produce subjective reality.

Every coordinate in space contains the total information of all other coordinates. The structure mirrors physicist David Bohm's implicate order: an enfolded holographic sea of infinite potential from which observable phenomena unfold.

In a state Grinberg called no-self — achieved through deep meditation or shamanic practice — the individual ego dissolves, consciousness merges with the Lattice, and intentional reality-shaping becomes possible. This was not metaphor. He attempted to measure it.

▸ ANOMALY — SUBJECT MISSING // CASE SUPPRESSED

Grinberg was last seen on 8 December 1994 — the same month his landmark paper was published. He told his daughter he was traveling to Kathmandu for meditation sessions. No airline has a record of him boarding any flight.

His second wife, Teresa Mendoza, disappeared the same day. When investigators entered the residence, all research materials were gone — computers, files, writings: erased.

Comandante Clemente Padilla, the assigned investigator, was removed from the case under pressure from Mexico's Attorney General's Office. A second investigator contradicted Padilla's findings. President Ernesto Zedillo personally requested information on the file. The investigation was called off. Padilla chose retirement rather than compliance.

Grinberg's brother brought the full archive to the police chief hoping to restart the investigation. Three days later, the file had vanished from the police chief's office.

CIA Stargate program documents — declassified August 2023 — reference Grinberg's studies explicitly. The Stargate program was terminated in 1995. One year after Grinberg disappeared.

Who is Jacobo Grinberg
Born in Mexico City in 1946, Grinberg developed a fascination with the human mind from an early age. At just 12 years old, he witnessed his mother die from a brain tumour, and from that point on he decided he would study the human brain. UNILAD Tech He pursued higher education at the National Autonomous University of Mexico (UNAM), earning a bachelor's degree, then a master's at New York Medical College, and a PhD in psychophysiology from UNAM's Faculty of Sciences, with additional training at New York University's Brain Research Laboratory. Grokipedia He then returned to Mexico and founded his own laboratory at the Universidad Anáhuac. Later, in 1987, Grinberg founded the Instituto Nacional para el Estudio de la Conciencia (National Institute for the Study of Consciousness), where he began to merge his scientific expertise with studies into meditation, shamanism, telepathy, and their connection to brain activity. Core Claims & Theories
